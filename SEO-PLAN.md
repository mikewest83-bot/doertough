# Mike AI SEO Plan

Mike AI is positioned first as a conversational AI companion and second opinion. Money, negotiation, affordability, and real-estate intelligence are capabilities, not the entire identity.

## Safe rollout
- Technical metadata and canonical homepage first.
- Add search-intent pages only after routing and rendered behavior are verified.
- Keep SEO changes isolated from realtime, billing, database, and deployment infrastructure.
- Do not use mass-produced keyword pages or manipulative link tactics.

## Priority questions
- Should I buy this?
- Am I getting a good deal?
- Can I afford this?
- How should I negotiate this?
- What am I missing?
- How can I save money?
- Can someone help me think this through?
