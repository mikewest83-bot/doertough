# Mike AI SEO Plan

## Positioning
Mike AI is positioned first as a conversational AI companion and second opinion. Money, negotiation, affordability, and real-estate intelligence are important capabilities, not the entire identity.

Primary message: **Talk to Mike. Your AI companion and second opinion.**

## Phase 1 — Technical foundation
- Unique title and meta description
- Canonical homepage
- Robots directives and sitemap
- Open Graph and Twitter metadata
- WebSite, Organization, and SoftwareApplication structured data
- Verify direct URL routing before adding deep landing pages

## Phase 2 — Search-intent pages
Planned pages:
- /ai-companion
- /second-opinion-ai
- /voice-ai-assistant
- /save-money-with-ai
- /ai-negotiation-assistant
- /ai-real-estate-analysis
- /what-can-mike-do

Each page must provide genuinely useful, original content and a clear path into Mike.

## Phase 3 — Content
Prioritize useful questions people actually ask:
- Should I buy this?
- Am I getting a good deal?
- Can I afford this?
- How should I negotiate this?
- What am I missing?
- How can I save money?
- Can someone help me think this through?

Avoid mass-produced keyword pages and manipulative link tactics.

## Release rule
SEO changes remain isolated from product/realtime work until routing, build, tests, rendered behavior, and production safety are verified.
