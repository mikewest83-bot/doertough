Refund Policy

Last updated 26 August 2026 · Mike AI is operated by Doer Tough.

The free trial

Mike AI starts with 7 days free, and no card is required to start. If you never add a payment method, the subscription simply ends when the trial does and you are never charged. If you do add one, cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged. You can cancel through the Stripe billing portal without contacting support.

After the trial

Subscriptions bill monthly at $24.99. You can cancel any time, and access continues through the end of the period you've already paid for. We don't automatically refund partial months.

When we will refund

  • You were charged after successfully cancelling.
  • You were charged twice for the same billing period.
  • The service was substantially unavailable or broken for a meaningful part of your billing period.
  • You were charged after the free trial and contact us within 14 days of that charge to request a refund because you intended to cancel before the trial ended.

Contact mike.west83@gmail.com using the email address on your account. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method and typically appear within 5–10 business days after they are issued, depending on the payment provider.

How to cancel

Open Mike AI and click Manage billing. That opens Stripe's billing portal, where you can cancel your subscription or update your payment method.

This document is a starting point written for a small product, not legal advice. Consumer protection rules in some places may give customers additional cancellation or refund rights.
