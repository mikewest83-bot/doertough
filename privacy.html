Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 August 2026 · Mike AI is operated by Doer Tough.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your name and email address. Your password is stored only as a bcrypt hash — we cannot read it.
  • Your conversations. The messages you send and the responses Mike gives, so you can pick up where you left off.
  • Usage records. When you start a voice conversation, so we can apply plan allowances.
  • Basic technical logs. IP address, browser type, and error details, kept for security and debugging.

We do not store card numbers. Payments run through Stripe, which handles card details directly.

Who we share it with

ProviderWhat they receiveWhy
OpenAIYour messages and conversation historyGenerating Mike's responses
ElevenLabsYour voice audio and the text of responsesSpeech recognition and Mike's voice
StripeYour email and payment detailsSubscriptions and billing
RailwayApplication data and logsHosting and the database

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Account details and conversations are kept while your account exists. Technical logs are kept for a short period for debugging. Ask us to delete your account and we'll remove your account record and your conversations.

Your choices

  • Ask for a copy of what we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct it.
  • Ask us to delete your account and its data.
  • Cancel your subscription at any time from the billing portal.

Email mike.west83@gmail.com for any of these.

Security

Traffic runs over HTTPS, passwords are hashed with bcrypt, and access to the database is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't reuse elsewhere.

Children

Mike AI is not intended for anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app or by email.

This document is a starting point written for a small product, not legal advice. If you have customers in the EU, UK, or California, have a lawyer check it against GDPR and CCPA requirements before relying on it.
